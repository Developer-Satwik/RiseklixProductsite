## What The Attached Research Says

### FromSEOtoAnswerEngineOptimizationAEOGenerativeAiandtheTransformationofSearchVisibility.pdf

Use this as the strategic frame. It argues that visibility is shifting from ranked blue links to answer-layer presence, source selection, and zero-click influence. It also reinforces that Google says foundational SEO remains the base for AI Overviews and AI Mode.

Implementation implication:
- Keep technical SEO clean.
- Measure more than clicks: answer impressions, citations, source appearances, brand mentions, and assisted conversions.
- Create content that can be selected as a source, not just ranked as a page.

### two.pdf - GEO: Generative Engine Optimization

This is the strongest academic base. It finds that content visibility in generative engines can improve through citations, expert quotations, statistics, fluency, and authoritative presentation. It also shows keyword stuffing performs poorly.

Implementation implication:
- Every major resource page should include verifiable stats, cited sources, expert commentary, and original analysis.
- Do not publish generic AI-written SEO pages.
- Build citation-worthy evidence pages.

### one.pdf - Role-Augmented Intent-Driven G-SEO

This paper is useful for the content workflow. Its RAID G-SEO framework uses summarization, intent inference, multi-role reflection, step planning, and rewriting.

Implementation implication:
- Before writing a page, infer the buyer roles: founder, creator, coach, B2B marketer, ecommerce operator, SaaS team, consultant, podcast host.
- Use "Who / What / Why / How" to map buyer intent.
- Optimize each page around the likely prompts buyers ask, not just one keyword.

### three.pdf - From Search Intent To Retrieval Demand

This paper introduces Grounding Necessity: AI systems are more likely to retrieve external sources when the answer needs freshness, evidence, comparison, specificity, or current market context.

Implementation implication:
- Prioritize content where AI needs a source: benchmarks, checklists, current comparisons, platform changes, pricing frameworks, country/context differences, and original experiments.
- Avoid publishing only evergreen generic definitions.

### 2602.02961v1.pdf - Pinterest GEO

This is the multimodal lesson. It argues that visual content must be described by what users would search for, not merely what the image literally contains. It also supports topical collections and internal linking.

Implementation implication:
- Portfolio images/reels need intent-aligned captions and surrounding text.
- Build collection pages for "content systems", "podcast repurposing", "founder authority", "AI search visibility", and "client acquisition".
- Use videos and portfolio assets as searchable proof, not decorative media.

### 2603.29979v1.pdf - Structural Feature Engineering For GEO

This paper argues that structure itself affects citation behavior: macro-structure, meso-structure, and micro-structure. It reports consistent citation gains from better structure.

Implementation implication:
- Use clear page architecture, self-contained sections, concise answer blocks, comparison tables, evidence boxes, and cross-reference links.
- Avoid giant unstructured essays.
- Each resource page should be easy for humans and machines to parse.

### Optimizing-for-the-Artificial-Intelligence-Driven-Search-Era...

This paper supports the integrated model:
- SEO = technical and discoverability foundation.
- AEO = direct-answer visibility.
- GEO = inclusion and citation in AI-generated responses.

Implementation implication:
- Do all three together.
- Do not build separate silos for SEO, AEO, and GEO.

### Pasted ZipTie Article

Useful practitioner lens, but treat some figures as vendor/industry claims unless independently verified. Strong useful ideas:
- AI citation is not the same as Google ranking.
- Query fan-out matters.
- Citation/mention tracking needs prompt variants, not one fixed query.
- Content needs structural, verification, and authority confidence.

Implementation implication:
- Use it for tactical inspiration, not as the only evidence base.
- Build a prompt-monitoring set with paraphrases across buyer personas and countries.

### aeo geo (1).pdf - Paraphrase Brittleness

This is important for measurement. Prompt wording can dramatically change which brands AI systems recommend.

Implementation implication:
- Do not track "AI visibility" with one prompt.
- Use prompt clusters: cosmetic variations, persona variations, geography variations, specificity variations, and buying-stage variations.

## Online Research Confirmed

### Google

Google's current guidance says SEO remains relevant for generative AI features because AI Overviews and AI Mode are rooted in Google's ranking and quality systems. Google also explains query fan-out and RAG, but warns against treating AEO/GEO hacks as special Google requirements.

Practical takeaways:
- Make content crawlable, indexable, useful, original, and people-first.
- Use structured data where it genuinely describes visible content, but do not overfocus on schema as a magic AI lever.
- `llms.txt` is fine for other systems, but Google says it does not help or hurt Google Search.
- Tiny artificial chunking is not required for Google; structure should serve readers first.
- FAQ rich results no longer appear in Google Search as of May 7, 2026. FAQs can stay because they help users and AI extraction, but not for old FAQ dropdown SERPs.

### Bing / Copilot

Bing Webmaster Tools now exposes AI visibility reporting, including AI citations, grounding queries, intents, topics, citation share, and comparisons over time.

Practical takeaways:
- Bing is ahead in AI citation transparency.
- Set up Bing Webmaster Tools and use it for page-level citation learning.
- Organize content around topics and intent clusters, not only keywords.

### OpenAI / Perplexity Crawlers

OpenAI documents OAI-SearchBot for ChatGPT search surfaces and GPTBot for training. Perplexity documents PerplexityBot for search/result surfacing and Perplexity-User for user-triggered browsing.

Practical takeaways:
- Allow OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot if AI-search visibility is the goal.
- Do not block useful AI search crawlers unless there is a clear content-protection reason.
- Track AI crawler hits in logs later.
