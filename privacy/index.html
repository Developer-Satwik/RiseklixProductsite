Privacy Policy

How Riseklix Media collects, uses, protects, and manages personal information across our website, contact forms, analytics, and Rise Weekly newsletter.

Last updated: June 1, 2026

1. Who we are

Riseklix Media is a growth and content systems agency helping brands, founders, creators, and B2B teams turn attention into measurable business outcomes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage personal information when you visit riseklix.com, contact us, request a strategy call, subscribe to Rise Weekly, or interact with our services.

For privacy questions, data requests, or newsletter support, contact us at contact@riseklix.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to share with us, information generated when you use our website, and limited information received through service providers that help us run the business.

Information you provide directly

  • Name, email address, phone number, company name, role, website, and social profile links.
  • Messages, project briefs, budget ranges, goals, content references, brand assets, and any files or links you submit.
  • Strategy call details, scheduling preferences, and communication history.
  • Newsletter sign-up details, including email address, consent status, source, and subscription preferences.

Information collected automatically

  • Device, browser, IP address, pages viewed, referring pages, approximate location, session activity, and website interaction data.
  • Analytics and performance data from tools such as Google Analytics, tag managers, pixels, server logs, and similar technologies used to understand site performance and improve user experience.

Information from third-party platforms

If you contact us through LinkedIn, Instagram, X, email, Calendly, beehiiv, or another platform, we may receive the profile, message, or subscription information made available through that platform.

3. How we use personal information

We use personal information to operate the website, respond to inquiries, deliver services, and improve the way Riseklix communicates with potential clients and subscribers.

  • Respond to messages, proposals, audits, and strategy call requests.
  • Understand your brand, goals, audience, and service requirements.
  • Prepare content, social, ads, AI visibility, outreach, podcast, or growth-system recommendations.
  • Send Rise Weekly newsletter issues, updates, and related editorial or commercial communications when you subscribe.
  • Measure website performance, diagnose technical issues, protect the site, and prevent misuse.
  • Maintain business records, invoices, project history, consent logs, and compliance documentation.
  • Improve our website, positioning, content, services, and client onboarding experience.

4. Newsletter and Rise Weekly

When you subscribe to Rise Weekly, your email address and consent information may be processed through beehiiv, our newsletter platform. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us.

We only want newsletter subscribers who have intentionally opted in. Do not submit someone else’s email address without their permission.

5. Cookies, analytics, and tracking technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, tags, analytics scripts, and similar technologies to understand traffic, measure campaign performance, improve pages, and protect the site from abuse. These tools may collect device and usage information such as page views, session activity, browser type, approximate location, and referral source.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features, analytics accuracy, or form behavior.

6. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers and partners who help us operate our website, newsletter, analytics, scheduling, client communication, payments, hosting, automation, project management, or service delivery.

These providers may include website hosting platforms, analytics providers, newsletter platforms, email providers, scheduling tools, payment processors, cloud storage platforms, CRM tools, subcontractors, and professional advisers where necessary.

7. Client materials and project data

If you become a client, we may process brand assets, creative references, platform access information, campaign data, social media insights, audience data, content calendars, ad account information, and performance reports. We use this information only for agreed services, internal quality control, reporting, and business administration unless a written agreement says otherwise.

8. Legal bases and legitimate reasons

Depending on where you are located, our basis for processing personal information may include your consent, performance of a contract, steps taken before entering a contract, legitimate business interests, legal obligations, or protection against fraud and misuse.

9. International processing

Riseklix may work with clients, platforms, and service providers in multiple countries. Your information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence, including in jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Where required, we use reasonable safeguards to protect information during cross-border processing.

10. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including responding to inquiries, maintaining client records, sending newsletters, resolving disputes, improving services, complying with legal obligations, and maintaining business records. If you unsubscribe from Rise Weekly, we may retain limited information to maintain suppression records and prevent accidental re-subscription.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. No website, email system, or online service can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so please avoid sending sensitive passwords, payment credentials, or confidential platform access details through open forms or unsecured channels.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, export, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You may also withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

To make a privacy request, email contact@riseklix.com. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

13. Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for business users, founders, creators, and professional teams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

14. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party websites, social platforms, newsletter platforms, booking tools, portfolio assets, or Research pages. Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies, not this Privacy Policy.

15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, newsletter, analytics setup, or legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

16. Contact

For privacy questions, newsletter requests, or data requests, contact contact@riseklix.com.
