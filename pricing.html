Questions before paying

The details that should not require a sales call.

What can I do before paying?

You can start from your company, review the company context, discover relevant buying decisions, run initial AI recommendation checks, and see a useful priority finding before deciding whether the deeper workflow is worth paying for.

When does Pro make sense?

Pro makes sense when the first result creates a real next question: why did this happen, what evidence supports it, what should we change, did the change work, and what should we keep watching afterward?

Is Pro really $39 per month?

The public Pro price on this page is $39 per month. The app shows the current checkout amount, applicable taxes, eligibility, and exact plan limits before you purchase.

Why are exact usage limits not frozen on this page?

AI checks have changing provider and infrastructure costs. Riseklix keeps the marketing explanation stable while the in-app entitlement screen remains the current source of truth for exact limits and availability.

Do I need a sales call to start?

No. The product is designed to begin self-serve from your company. Marketing services and other support can remain separate from the software purchase.

Does paying for Pro guarantee an AI system will recommend my company?

No. Third-party AI systems change and remain outside Riseklix's control. Pro gives you deeper evidence, implementation and rechecking tools; it does not manufacture a guaranteed recommendation.
