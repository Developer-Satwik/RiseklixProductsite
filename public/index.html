Questions

Before you open a case.

How is this different from an SEO audit?

An SEO audit checks how your pages rank in Google. A study records what AI tools actually say when your buyers ask for help, who they recommend instead of you, and which sources they relied on. The fixes follow from that evidence.

AI gives a different answer every time. Is this reliable?

Single answers vary. That's why we ask each buyer situation four ways across five AI tools, about 20 answers per situation. Patterns that hold across that many answers are the ones worth acting on. With monthly tracking, every question runs twice per tool, three times a month, so you see trends rather than one-off answers.

What do you need from me?

Your website, who you sell to, two or three competitors and the markets you care about. We agree the 10 buyer situations with you before we record anything.

Do you make the fixes too?

If you want us to. Implementation covers page rewrites, decision pages, articles and profile clean-up, quoted per scope. Many clients make the first fixes themselves.

Do you only do AI visibility work?

No. We also run market research and opportunity validation for startups and enterprises: demand, market size, history, how competitors execute, and pricing, ending in a go, pivot or not-yet call. Any study can include an AI discovery check of the category.

Can you work with us long term?

Yes. Many projects start with one study and grow into a research partnership: flagship reports under your name, monthly market and competitor briefs, resources and models your team keeps using, and the research behind new business lines, all from people who already know your market.

Does Businesses in ChatGPT replace this?

No. OpenAI's new dashboard counts a mention only when ChatGPT cites your website, and it covers ChatGPT alone. In our research, ChatGPT used the firm's own site in about a third of its recommendations. A study covers five AI tools, including Google AI Overviews, and shows the competitors and sources the dashboard doesn't.
