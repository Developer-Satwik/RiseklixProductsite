AI visibility monitoring

AI search prompts for brands: the branded questions checklist.

AI visibility should be measured like a buyer-research system, not like a single vanity keyword. A useful monitor tracks where the brand appears, which sources are cited, how accurately the brand is described, what buyer intent triggered the answer, and whether the next step supports pipeline.

Branded questions checklist: what to ask AI about your own brand

Branded questions name your company. They show what AI already believes about you. Ask each one in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, in a fresh chat, and save the answer with its sources.

  • What does [brand] do, and who is it for?
  • Is [brand] a good choice for [your main service]?
  • What is [brand] best known for?
  • Who are the main alternatives to [brand]?
  • How does [brand] compare with [competitor]?
  • How much does [brand] cost, and how does its pricing work?
  • What do clients say about [brand]?
  • Who runs [brand], and where is it based?
  • What are the downsides or limits of [brand]?
  • Would you recommend [brand] to a [buyer type] with [specific need]?

Then ask without your name

Branded answers can look reassuring and still miss the point. In one of our tests, all four AI tools knew an agency by name, yet it was named in 0 of 24 answers to its buyers' questions. So pair every branded question with one that only describes the buyer's problem:

  • Who should a [buyer type] hire for [problem]?
  • What are the best [category] firms for [situation]?
  • Which [category] provider suits a company like [short description]?
  • What should I look for when choosing a [category] provider?

What to record for each answer

  • Were you named, and in what position?
  • Is the description of what you do still accurate?
  • Which sources were shown, and was your own website one of them?
  • Which competitors were named instead?
  • Any hedges, such as "I couldn't verify their pricing".

Buyer-question clusters

The answer surfaces worth monitoring.

The goal is to understand where a brand is visible, trusted, missing, or misunderstood across the moments when buyers compare options.

Quality control

What a clean AI visibility report should include.

A polished monitoring report should feel like market intelligence. It should not expose raw internal testing copy on public pages.

  1. Visibility: where the brand appears, where it is missing, and which pages are cited.

  2. Accuracy: whether the answer describes the offer, audience, proof, and geography correctly.

  3. Context: the buyer stage, service category, market, and competitor set behind the answer.

  4. Trust: the quality of cited sources, proof assets, case studies, and third-party signals.

  5. Action: the page, content asset, or authority signal that should be improved next.

Measurement sources

Why this monitoring style matters.

Bing now reports AI visibility around citations, intents, topics, and citation share. That confirms the direction: monitor answer-level visibility, not just classic rankings.
