1. Who we are

Riseklix Media ("Riseklix", "we") is a research firm based in New Delhi, India. We study how AI tools recommend businesses, and we research markets for our clients. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit riseklix.com, send us an enquiry, subscribe to our research notes, or work with us.

For privacy questions or requests, email contact@riseklix.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry form. What you need, your website or idea, your work email, who you sell to, competitors you name, your main market and any notes you add. The form also records the page you sent it from and the page that referred you.
  • Research notes. Your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Messages. Anything you send us by email, LinkedIn or another platform, and our replies.
  • Project information. If you become a client, the details needed to scope, deliver and invoice the work.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, pages viewed, referring pages, approximate location and how you move through the site.
  • This comes from analytics tools such as Google Analytics, and from server logs.

Information from other platforms

If you contact us through LinkedIn, email or another platform, we receive the profile and message information that platform makes available.

3. Research data

Our studies record what AI tools say about businesses, and the public web pages those tools cite. They aren't designed to collect information about individuals. If a recorded answer happens to name a person, for example a founder, we keep it only as part of that recorded answer.

The questions we put to AI tools are worded as a buyer would ask them. We don't enter your confidential information into those tools.

4. How we use personal information

  • To reply to enquiries and send a scope and price.
  • To understand your business, buyers, competitors and markets so we can scope and deliver research.
  • To send research notes, if you subscribed.
  • To measure how the website is used, fix problems, protect the site and prevent misuse.
  • To keep business records, invoices, project history and consent records.
  • To improve the website and our services.

5. Research notes

If you subscribe, your email address is processed by the newsletter and form services we use, which currently include beehiiv and Netlify. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any email, or by contacting us.

We only want subscribers who chose to sign up. Don't submit someone else's email address without their permission.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and improve pages. It sets cookies and collects device and usage information such as page views, browser type, approximate location and referral source. The site also stores two small settings in your browser, which remember your motion preference and whether the opening animation has played. Those two don't identify you.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking them may affect analytics accuracy but won't stop you reading the site.

7. How we share information

We don't sell personal information. We share limited information with the service providers that help us run the business, and only so they can provide their service to us:

  • Netlify, which hosts this website and receives form submissions.
  • Google, for analytics.
  • beehiiv, for the research notes.
  • Email, cloud storage, payment and invoicing providers.
  • Subcontractors and professional advisers, where needed.

8. Client materials and confidentiality

If you become a client, we use the information you give us only for the agreed work, quality control, reporting and business administration, unless a written agreement says otherwise.

We publish findings from studies only in anonymised form. We name a client, or publish a case study that could identify one, only with that client's written permission.

9. Legal bases

Depending on where you are, our basis for processing personal information may be your consent, the performance of a contract, steps taken before entering a contract, our legitimate business interests, legal obligations, or protection against fraud and misuse.

10. International processing

We work with clients and service providers in several countries. Your information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence, including where our service providers operate. Where required, we use reasonable safeguards to protect information that crosses borders.

11. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected: replying to enquiries, keeping client records, sending research notes, resolving disputes, meeting legal obligations and keeping business records. If you unsubscribe, we may keep limited information so we don't subscribe you again by mistake.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure. No website, email system or online service is perfectly secure, so please don't send passwords, payment details or confidential access details through the form or by open email.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information. You can withdraw consent to marketing emails at any time.

To make a request, email contact@riseklix.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on some requests.

14. Children's privacy

This website and our services are for businesses and professionals. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we'll remove it.

15. Third-party links

This website links to other websites and platforms, including sources cited in our articles. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, not this one.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, our services, our tools or our legal obligations change. The updated version will be posted here with a new date.

17. Contact

For privacy questions, newsletter requests or data requests, email contact@riseklix.com.
