Open case file 003

What the three have in common

  • Each firm was established in its field. The problem was evidence. AI couldn't find the proof for the work the firm most wanted.
  • AI judged them job by job. The same firm could lead one buyer situation and vanish from the next.
  • The fixes were specific. For one firm it was pricing that AI couldn't verify. For another it was a page setting hiding a whole site.
