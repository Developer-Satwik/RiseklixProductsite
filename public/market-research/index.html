What we investigateSix questions, answered with sources

Demand

Is anyone actually looking?

Search behaviour, AI answers, forums, reviews and job posts that show a real problem and real budget.

Market size

How big is it, really?

A bottom-up estimate built from customer counts and prices, shown as a range with every assumption visible.

History

Who tried this before?

Past launches, funding, shutdowns and pivots, what killed them, and what has changed since.

Competitors

How do they execute?

Positioning, pricing pages, channels, hiring, product updates, reviews, and who AI assistants recommend.

Pricing

What will buyers pay?

Public price points, packaging patterns and switching costs, turned into a realistic price band.

Verdict

Go, pivot or not yet?

What would have to be true, the risks that could kill it, and the cheapest next test.
