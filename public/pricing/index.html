AI Discovery Study: from US$250

The study covers 10 buyer situations, each asked four ways, across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. That's about 200 recorded answers, with every source traced. You receive a private study site, a PDF and the fixes, ranked.

The price rises with scope, such as more buyer situations, more markets or deeper competitor work. We send a fixed quote within one working day of your enquiry, and nothing starts until you approve it. Studies are paid upfront.

See everything the study includes.

Monthly tracking: US$400 to US$600 a month

AI answers move. Tracking shows which changes are real.

  • Your agreed questions are tracked three times a month.
  • Each question runs twice per AI tool, across all five tools.
  • Each month you get competitor share, source changes and three fixes.

Implementation: quoted per scope

Once a study shows what's missing, we put it there. That goes well beyond page rewrites: decision pages, articles, LinkedIn and Reddit content, video, directory and review profiles, and press. Many clients make the first fixes themselves and bring us in for the rest.

See every way we get you cited.

Market research: quoted per scope

Idea validation, market sizing, market history, competitor teardowns and pricing research, ending in a go, pivot or not-yet verdict. The scope is fixed before we start. See how market research works.

Research partnership: quoted per scope, monthly

For teams that need research all year: flagship reports, monthly market and competitor briefs, resources and models, and the research behind new business lines. See the research partnership.

Partners: per project

Agencies and consultants use our research behind their own advice, delivered under their name. Tell us about the client and we'll scope it.

How payment works

  • You get a fixed scope and price by email before anything starts.
  • Studies are paid upfront once you approve the scope.
  • Tracking and partnerships are billed monthly.

What we won't promise

  • A ranking. AI answers don't have one.
  • Guaranteed placement in any AI tool.
  • That money buys recommendations. OpenAI says paying doesn't change them.

Questions

How much does an AI Discovery Study cost?

A study starts at US$250, paid upfront once you approve the scope. Larger scopes, such as more buyer situations or more markets, are quoted before anything starts.

How much is monthly tracking?

US$400 to US$600 a month. Your agreed questions are tracked three times a month, with two runs per AI tool, across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Why is market research quoted per scope?

Because the work depends on the question. Checking demand for one idea is a different job from sizing a market across several regions. We agree a fixed scope and price before we start.

Do you offer a free audit?

No. You can read the case files and guides to see how the work looks, and you get a fixed scope and price before paying anything.

Do you make the fixes too?

If you want us to. Implementation goes well beyond page rewrites: decision pages, articles, LinkedIn and Reddit content, video, directory and review profiles, and press, wherever the study shows evidence is missing. It's quoted per scope. Many clients make the first fixes themselves.
