What we do

  • AI discovery studies. We ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews the questions your buyers ask, trace every source behind every answer, and hand you the fixes. See the study.
  • Market research and idea validation. Demand, market size, history, how competitors execute and what buyers already pay, ending in a clear verdict. See market research.
  • Research partnerships. Ongoing research for teams that need it all year: reports, monthly briefs and the evidence behind new business lines. See the partnership.
  • Implementation. After a study, we produce the evidence that's missing: pages, articles, LinkedIn and Reddit content, video, profiles and press. See every way we get you cited.

Who we work with

Service businesses of every kind: recruiters, training firms, contractors, consultancies and agencies. Our published case files cover a UK recruitment firm, a US training firm and a US commercial contractor. Agencies and consultants also use our research behind their own advice.

Before we focused on research, Riseklix built content and social systems for experts and creators. That earlier work is where our production skills come from.

How we work

  • Every answer is read by a person. Satwik Kumar, the founder, reads every recorded answer before a study reaches a client.
  • Everything is checkable. You see exactly which questions we asked and what every tool said.
  • Findings are counts. We report a number of answers out of a stated total, and say what the study can't prove. Read the method.
  • Case files are anonymised. We name a client only with their written permission.
  • The scope is fixed first. You get the scope and the price by email before anything starts.

What we won't promise

  • A ranking. AI answers don't have one.
  • Guaranteed placement in any AI tool.
  • That money buys recommendations. OpenAI says paying doesn't change them.

Who runs it

Satwik Kumar founded Riseklix in 2024 and leads the research. Riseklix Media is based in New Delhi and works with clients remotely, by email and chat.
