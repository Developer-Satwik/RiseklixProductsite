These numbers belong to each project and its time window. They show the kind of result the work produced, not a promise for the next one.

What we made

  • Short-form video. Hook-first edits, captions and pacing built for Reels and Shorts, often cut from longer recordings.
  • Expert authority. Editorial planning and publishing on LinkedIn and Instagram for specialists who had the knowledge but no public rhythm.
  • Repurposing. One long video or podcast turned into many pieces, each native to its platform.
  • Outreach. Prospect research and personal messages, so attention led to real conversations.

You can watch a selection in the reel archive.

How this connects to the research

AI tools build their answers from what is published about a firm, on its own site and everywhere else. Knowing what's missing is the job of a study. Producing it well, on the page, on LinkedIn and on video, is the job of implementation. This earlier work is where we learned to do the second part.
