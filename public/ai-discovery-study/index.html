The short version

  • What it is. A recorded study of what five AI tools tell your buyers when they ask who to hire.
  • What it covers. 10 buyer situations, each asked four ways, across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. About 200 answers.
  • What you get. A private study site, a PDF, and the fixes ranked by what to change first.
  • What it costs. From US$250, paid upfront once you approve the scope.

What the study answers

Buyers now ask an AI tool for a shortlist before they visit a website or call anyone. The study records those answers and reads every one, so you can see three things.

  • Are you recommended? For which buyer situations, by which tools, and how often.
  • Who is recommended instead? The firms that appear in your place, and the pages that earned them the mention.
  • What is the answer built on? The sources each tool leaned on: your own site, a directory profile, a review page or a competitor's pricing page.

How it runs

  1. Intake. You send your website, who you sell to and two competitors.
  2. Situations. We agree 10 buyer situations with you, with four questions for each.
  3. Recording. We ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, and trace every source.
  4. The file. You get the study site, the PDF and the ranked fixes.

The scope is fixed and agreed before we record anything. Read the full method, including how we count and what a study can't tell you.

Who it's for

Service businesses whose buyers ask around before they hire: recruiters, training firms, contractors, consultancies, agencies and other firms that win work through a shortlist.

It isn't for anyone who wants a guaranteed position in an AI answer. Nobody can sell that honestly.

Questions

How is this different from an SEO audit?

An SEO audit checks how your pages rank in Google. A study records what AI tools actually say when your buyers ask for help, who they recommend instead of you, and which sources they relied on. The fixes follow from that evidence.

AI gives a different answer every time. Is this reliable?

Single answers vary. That's why we ask each buyer situation four ways across five AI tools, about 20 answers per situation. Patterns that hold across that many answers are the ones worth acting on. With monthly tracking, every question runs twice per tool, three times a month, so you see trends rather than one-off answers.

What do you need from me?

Your website, who you sell to, two or three competitors and the markets you care about. We agree the 10 buyer situations with you before we record anything.

Do you make the fixes too?

If you want us to. Implementation goes well beyond page rewrites: decision pages, articles, LinkedIn and Reddit content, video, directory and review profiles, and press, wherever the study shows evidence is missing. It's quoted per scope. Many clients make the first fixes themselves.

Does Businesses in ChatGPT replace this?

No. OpenAI's dashboard counts a mention only when ChatGPT cites your website, and it covers ChatGPT alone. In our research, ChatGPT used the firm's own site in about a third of its recommendations. A study covers five AI tools, including Google AI Overviews, and shows the competitors and sources the dashboard doesn't.
