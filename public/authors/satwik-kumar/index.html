What he works on

Riseklix records how ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews answer the questions buyers ask before they hire a service firm. Each recommendation is then traced back to the sources the tool relied on. Satwik designs the question sets, reads the answers and writes the findings. Read the method.

The same approach runs through the market research work: demand, market history, how competitors execute and what buyers already pay, ending in a clear go, pivot or not-yet call.

What a byline means here

A byline marks who is responsible for the framing, the choice of sources, the definitions, the calculations and the interpretation on a page.

  • Sources. Factual claims lean on primary platform documentation, official guidance, first-party research and clearly scoped independent studies. Third-party facts stay attributed to where they came from.
  • Our own numbers. Figures from Riseklix studies are counts of recorded answers, not estimates.
  • Corrections. If something on this site is wrong, email contact@riseklix.com and it will be fixed.
