What the work covers

Research the decision, preserve the evidence, then make the interpretation explicit.

Satwik's work at Riseklix focuses on how AI systems retrieve sources, describe companies, compare alternatives, form recommendations, and change over time. The main-site reference guides translate that work into testing protocols, formulas, buyer-decision frameworks, comparisons, and implementation rules.

Riseklix Research at ai.riseklix.com publishes the broader field-note archive, experiments, methodology, and market research. The main site focuses on durable reference pages connected to the Commercial Discovery product.

Authorship standard

What a Satwik Kumar byline means on Riseklix.

A byline identifies responsibility for the framing, source selection, definitions, calculations, and Riseklix-specific interpretation on the page. Third-party facts remain attributed to their original sources.

Source hierarchy

Prefer primary platform documentation, first-party research, public product pages, official guidance, and clearly scoped independent studies for factual claims.

Framework labels

Riseklix-created models are identified as proposed frameworks. They are not presented as universal standards simply because they are useful internally.

Dates that mean something

Published and reviewed dates change when the content is materially created or re-evaluated—not whenever the site deploys.

Corrections over certainty

AI products and source behavior change. Reference pages should preserve uncertainty, limitations, and dated verification rather than imply permanent ranking rules.
