Corrections policy

Correct the record without rewriting the history.

Send the study, row or claim, original source, and proposed correction to satwik@riseklix.com. Material corrections should state what changed and preserve the original observation date or version boundary.

Riseklix does not change a finding in exchange for a commercial relationship. A brand's inclusion in a study is not a claim that it is a client, partner, endorser, or approved supplier.

How the research is produced

Human responsibility stays visible even when tools assist the work.

Individual studies disclose their own method. Across the desk, public web research and AI-assisted extraction may be used to locate or organize source material. The named author remains responsible for framing, source selection, interpretation, limitations, and the published conclusion.

Primary source first

Use official platform documentation, original datasets, first-party product pages, regulators, and clearly scoped independent studies where possible.

Observation ≠ inference

A recorded page fact, a generated answer, and a Riseklix interpretation are kept separate when the distinction matters.

No invented denominator

If a study cannot observe the population or denominator required for a percentage, it should not manufacture one.

Version the change

Material method changes, source revisions, or new collection windows should be dated and described instead of silently overwriting the old study.

Commercial disclosure

Riseklix has an interest in this category.

Riseklix builds Commercial Discovery software and offers related research, visibility, and marketing work. That commercial interest should be considered when reading Riseklix interpretation, comparisons, or category analysis.

The mitigation is not pretending neutrality. It is publishing the method, primary sources, inconvenient observations, limitations, author responsibility, correction route, and enough underlying material for a skeptical reader to inspect the claim.
