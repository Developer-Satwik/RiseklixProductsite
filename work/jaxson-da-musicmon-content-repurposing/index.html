Starting point

The creator had authority, but not enough infrastructure around it.

A strong case study starts with the risk, not the victory lap. These were the gaps the system needed to solve.

01

Single-platform concentration

Most authority lived on YouTube, leaving Instagram and short-form discovery underdeveloped.

02

Dormant content value

Existing videos contained strong moments, but they were not being systematically repackaged for new audiences.

03

Commercial expansion

Audience trust existed, but Patreon, merchandise, and monetization ideas needed clearer routing.

The lesson for similar brands.
