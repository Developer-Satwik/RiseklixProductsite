Direct answer

What “best” should mean in the USA market.

“Best agency” is a buyer question, not a category that Google or an AI system can settle for every company. Use a transparent scorecard that connects the agency's method to your sales motion, geography, proof burden, and revenue model.

Best for national demand

Choose a partner that can turn one commercial question into a source page, supporting video, expert distribution, LinkedIn conversation, and measurable conversion path across the country.

Best for local demand

Look for genuine location evidence, eligible Business Profiles, service-area logic, reviews, local proof, and pages that answer a market need. A fake city page creates ambiguity rather than prominence.

Best for AI visibility

The partner should track prompts across Google generative features, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Copilot or Bing, and relevant social discovery. A mention without accurate citation is not a complete win.

Best for regulated trust

Financial, health, legal, and other high-consequence categories need claim review, context-rich proof, clear limitations, and an approval workflow. Speed is valuable only when accuracy survives it.

Best for founder-led brands

The system should make the founder's point of view legible: named expertise, repeatable frameworks, original examples, short-form video, long-form source content, and respectful LinkedIn outreach.

Best for pipeline

Ask for qualified opportunity rate, sales acceptance, influenced revenue, and time to opportunity. Impressions and AI mentions are leading indicators, not the commercial definition of success.

Original method

Use the PROOF test before you compare providers.

Our conclusion from the USA signals is simple: the most defensible provider is the one that can make the brand easy to interpret, easy to verify, and easy to route into a commercial next step. Score each pillar from 0 to 4 and keep evidence beside the score.

P / Presence

Can the partner improve crawlability, indexation, entity consistency, local eligibility, citations, and the places where the buyer already searches?

R / Relevance

Does it map real USA buyer prompts to the correct vertical, role, state, metro, urgency, and commercial language rather than chasing broad traffic?

O / Original proof

Will the output include original observations, client evidence with context, named expertise, useful comparisons, and sources that others can verify?

O / Omnichannel distribution

Can the same idea move from a canonical article to short-form, YouTube, LinkedIn, Substack, and relevant outbound without losing meaning?

F / Funnel attribution

Can the team connect prompt visibility and cited URLs to qualified sessions, form quality, sales acceptance, pipeline, and revenue influenced?

Decision rule

Do not select the highest deck score. Select the provider whose weakest PROOF pillar does not break your buying motion, and require a repair plan for the rest.

Measurement design

The USA KPI is accurate, qualified visibility.

Search Console generative AI data and standard search data answer different parts of the story. Join them with prompt monitoring, analytics, CRM source capture, and sales feedback; do not collapse them into one invented “AI ranking” number.
