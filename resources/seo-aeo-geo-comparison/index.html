Direct answer

Are AEO and GEO the same thing?

AEO and GEO overlap, but they are not identical. AEO focuses on making a page answer specific questions clearly. GEO focuses on making a brand or source useful enough for generative systems to summarize, include, or cite. SEO is still the foundation because the content has to be crawlable, indexable, fast, and trusted first.

What is AEO?

Answer engine optimization makes important questions easy to answer from the page: concise definitions, comparison tables, FAQ-style sections, clear service language, and direct next steps.

What is GEO?

Generative engine optimization makes the source more useful for AI-generated answers: original frameworks, current evidence, named proof, external corroboration, and consistent entity facts across the site and the wider web.

What is SEO in the AI era?

SEO still handles discovery and trust: technical access, canonical URLs, internal links, helpful content, page experience, and authority. Without that base, AEO and GEO work has little surface area to win.

Buyer intent

What should an AEO, GEO, or AI visibility package include?

A credible package should include an initial audit, a prioritized content map, technical cleanup, page rewrites, evidence improvements, internal linking, crawler access checks, and recurring AI share-of-voice reporting. Be careful with vendors who promise guaranteed AI citations or guaranteed rankings.

Audit

One-time audit

Best for teams that need the current state: indexed pages, duplicate URLs, answer gaps, citation gaps, content quality, and conversion routing.

Sprint

30 to 90 day visibility sprint

Best when the site has search signals but needs stronger pages, clearer answer blocks, proof assets, and internal links around one topic cluster.

Retainer

Monthly share-of-voice optimization

Best when the goal is sustained growth: publish useful assets, monitor AI citations, compare competitors, refresh dated pages, and feed insights back into sales and content.

Report

White-label or executive reporting

Best for agencies or teams that need repeatable AI visibility reporting across accounts, including prompts, cited pages, competitors, and next actions.
