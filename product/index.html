The direct answer

Riseklix is not a dashboard for counting mentions.

It is a workflow for understanding a commercial decision: whether AI includes your company, what shaped the answer, what you can change, and whether the same decision moves afterward.

01 / DISCOVER

Where are you actually in the decision?

See the individual AI answers, competitors, citations, and recommendation state tied to a buyer question that matters to your business.

02 / EXPLAIN

What evidence supports the gap?

Keep observation, sources, competitor context, and interpretation connected enough to inspect instead of trusting a single score.

03 / IMPROVE

What should change next?

Turn the clearest supported gap into an implementation path, then rerun the same decision after the work ships.

What stays separate

Mentioned. Cited. Recommended. First choice. Different events.

A commercial analysis should not collapse distinct answer behaviors into one unexplained number.

MENTIONYour brand appears in the answer.

Useful, but it does not prove the model selected you as a choice.

CITATIONYour page is used as a visible source.

You can be cited while another company receives the recommendation.

RECOMMENDATIONYour company is presented as a fit or choice.

This is closer to the commercial question Riseklix is built to inspect.

FIRST CHOICEYour company is named before alternatives.

A stronger selection signal, but still one observation inside a changing system.

Method matters

What Riseklix will not pretend.

Read the methodology ↗
01

AI systems change.

An answer is an observation at a point in time, not a permanent ranking.

02

Different surfaces can differ.

Provider/API measurements should not be presented as identical to every consumer-app experience.

03

No recommendation is guaranteed.

Riseklix can identify evidence and support a change; it cannot promise how a third-party AI system will respond afterward.

04

Quiet monitoring is not failed monitoring.

“No meaningful change found” is different from incomplete coverage or a provider error.

Questions before you start

Commercial Discovery, without the jargon.

Is Riseklix an AI visibility tool?

AI visibility is part of it. Riseklix also models buying decisions, preserves recommendation and citation evidence, turns supported gaps into action plans, and separates deliberate Rechecks from ongoing monitoring.

Do I need to choose prompts first?

No. Riseklix starts by researching the business and the buying decisions that matter, then builds relevant questions from that context for review and approval.

Why not just track hundreds of prompts?

Large prompt sets can be useful for broad visibility measurement. Riseklix prioritizes commercially relevant questions so the evidence, finding, implementation, and before/after result can stay connected.

What happens after I make a change?

Recheck reruns the approved baseline questions so you can compare the result against the same panel. Pulse handles ongoing changes separately.

Does Riseklix guarantee AI recommendations?

No. Third-party AI systems change and remain outside Riseklix's control. The product is designed to make observations, evidence, implementation, and rechecking more rigorous—not to manufacture a guaranteed outcome.
