What makes it different

The unit of analysis is the buying decision.

Riseklix does not reduce a company to a keyword list. It models the commercial situation around the question.

Business-first discovery

Questions are generated from your company, offer, buyers, constraints, geography, and market—not from a generic prompt database alone.

Evidence-first recommendations

Observations, citations, competitor context, interpretation, and recommended work remain distinct enough to inspect.

Fixed-panel rechecks

After a change, rerun the same approved questions so before/after movement has a stable reference point.

Ongoing monitoring

Pulse watches meaningful movement between deliberate rechecks without pretending every small change deserves attention.
